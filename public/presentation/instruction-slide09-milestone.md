# Slide 09 — Milestone Timeline Animation Instruction

## Objective

Enhance **Slide 09** with a structured **progressive milestone timeline animation** that communicates the real project journey of LogKerja.

This slide must show that the project:
- started from a real operational problem;
- progressed through validated development stages;
- reached implementation milestones in a logical sequence;
- is currently implemented in a limited real-world scope.

The animation must feel:
- chronological;
- clear;
- deliberate;
- professional;
- easy to follow while presenting.

Do not change:
- slide background;
- existing text content;
- typography style;
- colour palette;
- top-right slide label;
- bottom navigation;
- overall visual identity.

---

## Core Animation Principle

This slide must behave like a **left-to-right project journey**.

The audience should see:
1. the headline first;
2. the timeline baseline appear;
3. each milestone activate one by one from left to right;
4. the February 2026 milestones animate as a compressed but readable cluster;
5. the current-scope box appear only after the full milestone journey completes.

The timeline must **not** reveal all seven milestones at once.

---

## Confirmed Milestones

Show **only** these seven milestones:

1. **Idea Generation** — April 2025
2. **Prototype Development** — Disember 2025
3. **MVP** — Februari 2026
4. **System Testing** — Februari 2026
5. **Go Live** — Februari 2026
6. **Continuous Improvement** — Mac 2026
7. **HKIP Submission** — Mei 2026

Do not add any extra milestone, phase, or placeholder stage.

# Part B — Timeline Progression

## General Behaviour
The milestone journey must progress from **left to right**.

At any given moment:
- the current milestone receives the strongest emphasis;
- already completed milestones remain visible as completed;
- upcoming milestones remain subdued until reached.

Each milestone activation must include:
- node pulse;
- title brightening;
- date reveal or sharpening;
- a short hold before moving to the next milestone.

---

## Milestone Visual States

### Inactive state
Before activation:
- node ring visible but subdued;
- inner dot subdued;
- milestone title dimmed;
- milestone date dimmed or soft;
- baseline visible but understated.

### Current active state
When a milestone is being reached:
- outer ring brightens cyan;
- inner dot pulses;
- title becomes brighter and more readable;
- date fades in or sharpens;
- progress line reaches this node and pauses briefly.

### Completed state
After the milestone is passed:
- node remains active/completed;
- completed portion of the progress line remains cyan;
- title and date remain visible;
- completed nodes may reduce emphasis slightly relative to the current active node, but must still clearly appear achieved.

---

## Step 1 — Idea Generation
When the timeline progression begins:

1. The cyan progress line starts from the far-left beginning of the baseline.
2. It grows or travels to **Idea Generation**.
3. When it reaches node 1:
   - node 1 pulses cyan;
   - **Idea Generation** brightens;
   - **April 2025** fades in or sharpens;
   - hold briefly.

The operational problem was first identified here.

---

The identified operational need was translated into a testable solution.

## Step 3 — February 2026 Milestone Cluster
This part contains three confirmed milestones in the same month:
- **MVP**
- **System Testing**
- **Go Live**

These three milestones must be treated as a **cluster progression**.

### Required behaviour
The progress line must:
1. move to **MVP**;
2. activate **MVP**;
3. move a short distance to **System Testing**;
4. activate **System Testing**;
5. move a short distance to **Go Live**;
6. activate **Go Live**.

### Required timing feel
This February 2026 sequence must feel like a **rapid progress burst**, but remain readable.

Do not make it too slow like the earlier milestones, and do not make it too fast to read.

### Activation behaviour for each February milestone
For each of the three nodes:
- the progress line reaches the node;
- the node pulses cyan;
- the title brightens;
- the date **Februari 2026** becomes clear;
- hold briefly before continuing.

Meaning:
February 2026 marks a concentrated period of major project advancement.

# Part C — Required Polish Behaviour

The following three polish behaviours are **required**, not optional.

## Polish 1 — Progress Line Must Fill the Journey
The timeline must use a clear **cyan progress line** that visually fills the journey from left to right.

Required behaviour:
- the timeline baseline exists across the whole row;
- the portion of the line already traversed becomes cyan;
- the portion not yet reached remains subdued;
- the audience must always be able to tell:
  - what has already been achieved;
  - what is currently active;
  - what is not yet reached in the reveal.

This progress line is part of the storytelling and must not be omitted.

---

## Polish 2 — February 2026 Cluster Must Feel Like a Burst
The **MVP → System Testing → Go Live** trio must visually feel like a more compact progression than the other nodes.

Required behaviour:
- the distances are shorter and the timing is slightly tighter;
- each node still gets its own pulse and readable activation moment;
- the cluster must communicate a burst of major progress during the same month;
- do not collapse the three milestones into a single grouped label;
- keep all three as separate nodes.

---

## Polish 3 — Final Completed State Must Settle Cleanly
After all milestones complete and the current-scope box appears:
- the full cyan progress line remains visible;
- all seven nodes remain in completed state;
- all titles and dates remain readable;
- the slide performs one subtle, calm “settle” emphasis;
- then the whole slide holds still.

Acceptable settle emphasis:
- a soft overall glow on the completed timeline;
- or a single subtle brightness settle across the timeline.

Do not create repeated flashing.

---

# Part E — Replay Behaviour

## Autoplay
- The full slide animation must autoplay **once** when Slide 09 is entered.
- It must **not** auto-loop.

## Manual replay control
Add a small replay / refresh icon button for the timeline sequence.

### Placement
Place the replay button neatly near the timeline content area, for example:
- below the timeline and above the scope box; or
- aligned neatly near the lower-right of the animated timeline region.

The placement must not disturb the layout.

### Button behaviour
When clicked, the replay button must:
1. reset the timeline progression to its initial state;
2. reset the cyan progress line;
3. reset node active/completed states;
4. hide or reset the scope box;
5. replay the milestone journey and scope reveal.

### Important replay rule
Do **not** replay the section label or headline.
Replay only:
- timeline baseline progression;
- milestone activations;
- scope box reveal.

# Part F — Timing Guidance

The exact timings may be adjusted slightly, but the feel must remain clear.

Recommended structure:
- Section label: `0.0–0.4s`
- Headline beat 1: `0.2–0.7s`
- Headline beat 2: `0.45–0.95s`
- Headline beat 3: `0.7–1.2s`
- Timeline baseline reveal: `1.2–1.7s`
- Idea Generation: `1.7–2.6s`
- Prototype Development: `2.6–3.5s`
- MVP: `3.5–4.1s`
- System Testing: `4.1–4.6s`
- Go Live: `4.6–5.1s`
- Continuous Improvement: `5.1–6.0s`
- HKIP Submission: `6.0–6.9s`
- Scope box reveal: `7.0–7.8s`
- Final settle state: `7.8–9.0s`

### February cluster timing guidance
Use slightly compressed timing for:
- MVP
- System Testing
- Go Live

Suggested feel:
- quick connector movement;
- short readable pause on each node;
- total cluster duration tighter than the earlier stages.
