# Slide 11 — Impact: Workforce Requirement Reduction Animation Instruction

## Objective

Enhance **Slide 11** with a clear before/after animation that communicates the operational impact of LogKerja on daily document monitoring workload.

This slide must show that:
- before LogKerja, monitoring required **4 officers**;
- after LogKerja, monitoring can be handled by **1 officer (Penyemak)**;
- the key meaning of this change is **reduced time and reduced manual follow-up effort**, not merely a raw staff count reduction.

The animation must feel:
- clear;
- structured;
- presentation-friendly;
- left-to-right;
- symbolic rather than dramatic.

Do not change:
- slide background;
- existing text content;
- typography style;
- colour palette;
- overall layout concept;
- top-right slide label;
- bottom navigation;
- overall visual identity.

---

## Core Animation Principle

This slide should communicate:

> **4 operational inputs consolidate into 1 clearly responsible role.**

Do **not** treat this as a literal headcount countdown animation.

The main visual story must be:
1. show the original four-person monitoring requirement;
2. visually consolidate that effort;
3. direct the result into one clearer responsible monitoring role;
4. then reveal the explanatory impact box on the right.

---

## Main Visual Story

This slide is not about “people disappearing.”
It is about **consolidation of effort and clearer responsibility**.

Therefore:
- avoid turning the number `4` into `3`, `2`, `1`;
- use the **four-dot convergence** as the main storytelling device;
- maintain calm, clean motion;
- make the direction consistently move **left to right**.

---

# Part B — Left-Side Workforce Animation

## Layout Elements to Animate
The animated comparison area contains:
- large number **4** with label **pegawai** on the left;
- arrow direction toward the right;
- large number **1** with label **pegawai** on the right;
- a **Sebelum** card with four small dots;
- a **Selepas** card with one dot.

This whole area should behave like a structured left-to-right transformation.

---

## Initial State
Before the workforce animation begins:
- the large number **4** is hidden;
- the large number **1** is hidden;
- the arrow is hidden;
- the **Sebelum** card is hidden;
- the **Selepas** card is hidden or present in an inactive state;
- the dots are hidden;
- the right-side impact meaning box is hidden.

After the headline and supporting line finish, start the left comparison sequence.

---

## Step 1 — Reveal “4 pegawai” and Sebelum card
Required sequence:
1. reveal the large number **4**;
2. reveal the label **pegawai** beside it;
3. reveal the **Sebelum** card;
4. keep the card in a calm idle state before the dots activate.

Suggested motion:
- fade in;
- slight upward movement;
- soft ease-out.

Meaning:
This establishes the original operational requirement.

---

## Step 2 — Activate the four dots in Sebelum
Inside the **Sebelum** card, animate the four dots one by one.

Required sequence:
1. first dot appears;
2. second dot appears;
3. third dot appears;
4. fourth dot appears;
5. after the fourth dot appears, the large number **4** pulses once.

Required visual behaviour:
- each dot should appear with subtle fade or scale-up;
- the motion must be clean and not playful;
- the four dots together clearly represent the four-person effort before implementation.

Meaning:
The original monitoring process involved four officers.

---

## Step 3 — Four-dot convergence
This is the core transformation moment.

The four dots from the **Sebelum** card must visually consolidate into a single continuing dot.

### Required behaviour
1. the four dots begin moving out from the Sebelum card area toward a common convergence point;
2. their movement is smooth and symbolic, not chaotic;
3. as they converge, three dots fade out or resolve away;
4. one remaining dot becomes the continuing dot;
5. this continuing dot then becomes the visual bridge toward the **Selepas** state.

### Important intent
This should look like **effort consolidation**, not staff elimination.

Do not create:
- explosive merging;
- dramatic collapsing;
- playful swirling;
- any movement that feels destructive.

The motion should be calm, geometric, and presentation-friendly.

Meaning:
The monitoring effort becomes more streamlined and directed.

---

## Step 4 — Arrow reveal and transition to “1 pegawai”
When the single continuing dot is formed:

1. reveal or draw the arrow toward the right;
2. move the single continuing dot along the arrow direction toward the **Selepas** side;
3. while this happens, reveal the large number **1** and the label **pegawai**;
4. reveal the **Selepas** card in an inactive state.

Required behaviour:
- the whole motion must move only from left to right;
- the arrow may sweep or draw itself once from left to right;
- the number **1** should not appear too early.

Meaning:
The process is now assigned to a more focused responsible role.

---

## Step 5 — Activate the Selepas state
When the continuing dot reaches the **Selepas** card:

1. place the dot inside the **Selepas** card;
2. pulse the dot once in green or green-cyan emphasis;
3. pulse the large number **1** once;
4. give the **Selepas** card a subtle border or glow emphasis;
5. keep the **Sebelum** side visible as reference.

Required final comparison state:
- **4 pegawai** remains visible on the left;
- **1 pegawai** remains visible on the right;
- Sebelum card shows four-dot state as the “before” reference;
- Selepas card shows one-dot state as the “after” result.

Meaning:
The monitoring responsibility becomes more focused and efficient.

---

### Placement guidance
Place it where it:
- is visible but discreet;
- does not disturb the layout;
- feels consistent with previous slides that use manual replay.

### Replay behaviour
When clicked, the replay button must:
1. reset the animated comparison area;
2. reset the dots;
3. reset the arrow and the numbers if appropriate;
4. reset the right-side impact box;

### Important replay rule

- workforce comparison animation;
- right-side impact box reveal.

# Part F — Required Polish Behaviours

The following polish items are **required**.

## Polish 1 — Do not animate `4` into `3, 2, 1`
Do **not** implement a literal number countdown for workforce.

Use the **four-dot convergence** as the primary storytelling mechanism.

Reason:
A numeric countdown could incorrectly suggest simple staff removal, while the actual message is operational consolidation.

---

## Polish 2 — Give the Selepas state clear ownership emphasis
When the single dot reaches the **Selepas** card:
- pulse the dot once;
- pulse the number **1** once;
- subtly emphasize the Selepas card border or glow.

Reason:
The visual should communicate that one clearly responsible role now owns the monitoring process.

---

## Polish 3 — Keep all motion left to right only
All movement in the main transformation must proceed from left to right.

Do not:
- reverse direction;
- bounce backward;
- orbit around;
- use random motion.

Reason:
The slide tells a story of simplification and direction.

---

## Polish 4 — Use colour with clear meaning
Apply colour intentionally:
- white/cyan = before/input/reference state;
- green = after/result state;
- cyan arrow = transformation path;
- avoid introducing unnecessary new colours.

Reason:
Colour should reinforce meaning, not decorate aimlessly.

---
